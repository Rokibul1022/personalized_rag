How Fine-Tuning Works with LoRA

What is Fine-Tuning?

Takes pre-trained Qwen 3B model and teaches it user-specific patterns without starting from scratch. Like a doctor specializing in pediatrics - doesn't forget medicine, just gets better at treating kids.

Triggers: Every 20 queries automatically

The Process Flow

20 Queries → Save to KB
↓
Load Qwen 3B + LoRA
↓
Train 3 Epochs (5-10 min)
↓
Save Adapters (50MB)
↓
Model Improved!

What Model Learns

  • Vocabulary: Age 10 = fun examples, Age 20 = technical depth
  • Topics: Math, physics, CS patterns
  • Style: Question format preferences
  • Format: Response length & detail level
